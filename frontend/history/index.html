THE ANNIVERSARY COLLECTION

A familiar home.
A year of growing.

From the first retained interface to today’s home console. The purpose stays the same: understand your computers, keep useful things close and choose what happens next.

This is an exhibit, not a second installation.

Every view uses synthetic examples. Historical controls cannot run, read your information or change TLI. The early edition comes from the earliest retained Git snapshot, v1.3.5-beta · May 3, 2026; it is not a claim about the first day of the project.

To use the early layout with current features, choose View → Classic UI in TLI’s header, or Settings → Appearance → Classic TLI. Color palettes remain a separate choice. Visiting this exhibit does not change your interface.
