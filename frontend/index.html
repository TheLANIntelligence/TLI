      Checking your Vault

      Checking whether this Vault is ready to open.

      Create a separate Vault passphrase in Security settings. Your TLI PIN controls access to TLI; the Vault passphrase opens its encrypted contents.

      Access opens for this TLI account for up to 30 minutes, including its other signed-in sessions.

      A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.

      TLI cannot reveal or replace it while preserving access to encrypted contents. Keep your original Vault data and installation if you may remember it later.

      Keep a copy of encrypted files

      This export includes encrypted files, Vault notes and this account’s secret store. It omits saved logins and Vault metadata, so it is not a complete, restorable backup.

      Reset an existing Vault

      Requires the current passphrase. Permanently deletes encrypted Vault data; this cannot be undone. Resetting does not recover a forgotten passphrase.

      Private files

      Keep a protected copy. Your original file stays where it is.
