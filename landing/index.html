macOS

Install on Mac in 60 seconds

No app store. No security warnings. One command does everything.

$ curl -fsSL https://thelanintelligence.com/install_mac.sh | bash

Paste this into Terminal — it downloads, installs, and launches TLI automatically.

Step 1
Open Terminal
Press ⌘ Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Return. A black or white window will open — that's Terminal.
Step 2
Paste the command
Click Copy above, then click inside Terminal and press ⌘ Cmd + V to paste. You'll see the command appear.
Step 3
Press Enter — done
Press Return. TLI downloads, installs to ~/TLI, and opens automatically in your browser. Takes about 30 seconds.
