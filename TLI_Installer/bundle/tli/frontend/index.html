⚠ Passphrase recovery is not possible

The passphrase is the only key that decrypts your data. There is no recovery mechanism — this is intentional. Your options:

1
Export encrypted backup
Downloads all encrypted vault files as a zip. Useless without the key — but if you ever remember your passphrase, you can restore and decrypt.
2
Hard reset vault
Permanently deletes all encrypted data and the password hash. You can then set a new passphrase. This cannot be undone.
